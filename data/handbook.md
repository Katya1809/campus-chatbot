# IIT Bhubaneswar Student Handbook & Fresher's Guide

## 1. Overview and Student Culture
Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar (IIT BBS) is a residential institute located in Argul, Jatni. Student life is driven by the Students' Gymkhana, comprising three elected councils (Science and Technology, Socio-Cultural, and Sports), specialized cells (CDC, Counselling, Alumni), and campus residents' welfare clubs.

---

## 2. Halls of Residence (Hostels)
- **First-Year Allocation**: Freshers are allotted to **Rushikulya Hall of Residence (RHR)** for boys and **Sangam Hall of Residence** for girls. These are among the newest hostel buildings on campus.
- **Single Occupancy**: All halls except Sangam offer single-occupancy rooms with private space.
- **Senior Hostels**:
  - Boys move to **Mahanadi Hall of Residence (MHR)** and **Brahmaputra Hall of Residence (BHR)**, or **Subarnarekha Hall of Residence (SHR)**.
  - Girls are promoted to **Ganga Hall of Residence (GHR)** for subsequent years.
- **Night Canteens**: Open daily from **6:00 PM onwards** in every hostel except SHR:
  - *RHR Night Canteen*: Popular for evening hot chai and pakoras.
  - *BHR Night Canteen*: Known for aloo/paneer parathas, rolls, and houses an on-site Amul Parlour.
  - *MHR Night Canteen*: Famous for biryani, fast food, and side dishes.
- **Hostel Governance**: Each hostel has an elected **General Secretary (GSec)**, Hall Office, and Warden Council to resolve room maintenance, mess, and facility issues.

## 3. Academic Schools & Departments
- **School of Electrical and Computer Sciences (SECS)**: Houses CSE, ECE, and EE. Located closest to student hostels. Features semiconductor fabrication and VLSI labs, computing hubs, and a dedicated SECS Mini Library.
- **School of Basic Sciences (SBS)**: Houses Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. Conducts courses for B.Tech Engineering Physics, Mathematics and Computing (MnC), and ITEP (B.Sc-B.Ed). Located right next to the Laboratory Complex (LBC) and Nescafe.
- **School of Mechanical Sciences (SMS)**: Home of "Royal Mech". Located towards the southern academic avenue; houses the **Design and Innovation Centre (DIC)** on the 3rd floor.
- **School of Infrastructure (SIF)**: Houses Civil Engineering and Engineering Drawing & Graphics (EDG) design studios; includes the SIF student canteen.
- **School of Earth, Ocean and Climate Sciences (SEOCS)**: Focuses on meteorological forecasting, atmospheric sciences, ocean modelling, and conducts the compulsory 1st-year ESTM course.
- **School of Minerals, Metallurgical and Materials Engineering (SMMME)**: Specializes in battery technologies, emerging materials, nanomaterials, and metallurgy; located adjacent to RHL.
- **School of Humanities, Social Sciences and Management (SHSSM)**: Conducts economics, ethics, philosophy, management, and B.Sc-B.Ed Economics. Undergraduates must take a minimum of **4 HSS breadth courses**.

## 4. Academic Complexes & Central Facilities
- **Lecture Hall Complex (LHC)**:
  - **Udayagiri Hall of Learning (UHL)**: Where the majority of first-year B.Tech and ITEP lectures are conducted.
  - **Lalitgiri Hall of Learning (LHL)**: Conducts SHSSM breadth courses and institute gatherings.
  - **Ratnagiri Hall of Learning (RHL)**: Large-format lecture halls for core departmental courses.
- **Laboratory Complex (LBC)**: Houses all first-year Physics, Chemistry, and Computing labs (except Introduction to Manufacturing Processes).
- **Design and Innovation Centre (DIC)**: Located on the 3rd floor of SMS; funds student prototype innovations, procures components, and supplies maker tools.
- **Central Library & Reading Room**:
  - Located on the **4th and 5th floors of the Main Administrative Building**.
  - **Library Timings**: Mon-Fri 8:30 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight); Weekends 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Book circulation counter closes at 8:30 PM.
  - **Reading Rooms**: Open 8:30 AM to 2:30 AM (open **24x7 during examination weeks**).

## 6. Extra Academic Activities (EAA)
Every first-year student is required to complete **one EAA stream** alongside a **compulsory Yoga course** (passing the yoga practical exam is mandatory):
1. **NSO (National Sports Organisation)**:
   - Covers Football, Cricket (boys), Basketball, Volleyball, Tennis, Badminton, Table Tennis, and Athletics.
   - Practice held **thrice weekly from 6:30 PM to 7:30 PM** at the Main Sports Complex.
   - Primary gateway for selection into the Inter-IIT sports contingents.
2. **NCC (National Cadet Corps)**:
   - Drill practice, camp training, and ceremonial march-pasts on Independence Day and Republic Day under Cadet In-Charge Sunita Ma'am.
3. **NSS (National Service Scheme)**:
   - Community outreach, rural school mentorship, plantation drives, and campus e-waste recycling. Classes held on weekends.

## 7. Course Structure, Grading & Attendance Policy
- **Structure**: Courses follow the **L-T-P (Lecture-Tutorial-Practical)** format with 1, 2, 3, or 4 credits.
- **Assessment Pattern**: Typically 20% Assignments/Surprise Quizzes, 30% Mid-Semester Examination, and 50% End-Semester Examination.
- **Crucial Attendance Policy**: Low attendance **does NOT deregister** a student from courses. Instead, attendance directly modifies your course total on a linear continuous scale from **-5 to +5 marks** (0% attendance penalizes -5 marks, while 100% attendance awards +5 bonus marks).
- **Grading Scale**: Ex (90-100), A (80-90), B (70-80), C (60-70), D (50-60), P (35-50, Pass), F (<35, Fail).
- **IMPORTANT RULE**: **There is NO branch change option at IIT Bhubaneswar**. Focus on maintaining a consistent CGPA above 7.5–8.0 without academic stress.
- **Compulsory 1st-Year Courses**:
  - *Semester 1*: Mathematics I (Calculus & ODE), BEEE (Basic Electrical & Electronics), Programming in C, ELC (English Language for Communication), HLS (Happiness & Life Skills - 1 credit based on attendance), ESTM (Environmental Science), IBT (Bioscience), IBKT (Bharatiya Knowledge Traditions).
  - *Semester 2*: Mathematics II (Linear Algebra & Complex Analysis), Engineering Mechanics (EM), branch-specific core introductory subjects.
- **Branch-Specific Introductory Courses**:
  - ECE: FSD (Fundamentals of Semiconductor Devices)
  - EE: ADEC (Analog and Digital Electronics Circuits)
  - CSE: DS (Discrete Structures)
  - MnC: DM (Discrete Mathematics)
  - EP: CMSR (Classical Mechanics and Special Relativity)

  - Operates scheduled loops connecting hostels (RHR/BHR/Sangam) to LHC and LBC before 8:30 AM and during lunch hours.
- **E-Rickshaws (On-Call Inside Campus)**:
  - Fast mobility between hostels and academic blocks.
  - Driver Contact Numbers: **+91 76839 95772**, **+91 89173 27084**.

  - Operating Hours: Daily **10:00 AM to 7:00 PM**.

## 9. Student Welfare, Cells & Support Bodies
- **Counselling Services Team**: Located on the 1st Floor of Sanjeevan Health Centre. Comprises a Faculty PIC, Professional Counsellor, elected Student Coordinators, Assistant Coordinators, and nominated Student Guides to help freshers handle academic stress, transition challenges, and personal well-being.
- **Career Development Cell (CDC)**: 2nd Floor Admin Building. Manages corporate internships from the 5th semester and final campus placements under the Training & Placement Officer.
- **Alumni Cell**: 2nd Floor Admin Building. Coordinates alumni networking, mentorship programs, and annual alumni meets.
- **Women Welfare Committee (WWC)**: Gender sensitization and redressal cell adhering to the POSH Act 2013 (+91 674 713 1400).
- **Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)**: Community development unit active in 6 adopted villages (Argul, Podapada, Kansapada, Khudupur, Padanpur, and Sundaria).
- **Sanjeevan Health Centre**: Near Sports Complex. Operates 24x7 with medical officers, emergency ambulance (+91 674 713 1100), and on-site Apollo pharmacy.
- **Campus Security Control Room**: Main Gate Argul (+91 674 713 4444).

## 10. Student Gymkhana Councils & Annual Fests
- **Technical Council Societies**: RISC (Robotics, Yanthrix), Neuromancers (Coding, ACM-ICPC), WebND (Web & UI/UX), Nakshatra (Astronomy, Galactic Cafe), SFEB (Finance & Economics).
- **Cultural Council Societies**: Aaroh (Music), D-Gang (Dance), The Fourth Wall (Dramatics), Kalakriti (Fine Arts), Cinewave (Films), Clix (Photography), Panacea (English Literary), Abhivyakti (Hindi Literary), Quiz Society.
- **Major Annual Fests**:
  - **Alma Fiesta**: Socio-cultural fest with pro-nites, star artists, music, dance, and fine arts.
  - **Wissenaire**: Techno-management fest with national robotics, coding challenges, exhibitions, and guest lectures.
  - **E-Summit**: Entrepreneurship summit by E-Cell with startup internship fairs and investor pitches.
  - **Pravaah**: Sports fest bringing university athletic teams together.
- **General Championship (GC)**: Inter-departmental tournament with points across Technical, Cultural, and Sports events.
- **Inter-IIT Meets**: Annual Inter-IIT Sports and Tech Meets; IIT Bhubaneswar co-hosted the 54th Inter-IIT Sports Meet.
